# Site Strategy

Site No.: 14
Repo: emfls-diy
Domain: https://diy.emfls.com/

## Mission

Help people who are new to making and repairing things prepare, measure, understand
their tools, and recognize when a job belongs with a qualified professional.

## Topic boundary

Cover beginner woodworking, furniture assembly and repair, painting, simple wall
work, tool use, material selection, and reading measurements. Exclude home-system
diagnosis, professional electrical/gas/structural work, hazardous machinery deep
dives, and product roundups made only to rank tools.

## Minimum information architecture

- `/` — the workshop starting point and a short pre-work checklist
- `/about/` — purpose, audience, and scope
- `/safety/` — stop conditions and safe-work boundaries
- `/editorial-policy/` — source and correction standards
- `/privacy/` and `/contact/` — minimum trust information
- `/404.html` — branded not-found response

Deeper projects, skill guides, and tools follow after the network-wide Foundation +
Search Launch wave. This small baseline comes from the Site Control Page and Owner
Directive 11; do not infer a broader content roadmap from the scaffold.
